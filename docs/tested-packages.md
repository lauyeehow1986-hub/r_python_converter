## 3. Popular Data Analysis Python Packages

Verified in [`tools/test-popular-analysis.R`](../tools/test-popular-analysis.R) and [`tools/test-domain-packages.R`](../tools/test-domain-packages.R):

- **`pymannkendall` (PyPI v1.4.2)**:
  - 13 exported statistical functions transpiled to R.
  - Verified non-parametric Mann-Kendall trend detection, p-value calculation, Sen's slope estimation, and tau correlation across monotonic and flat data.
- **`scikit-learn` & `statsmodels` OLS**:
  - Transpiled from [`corpus/sklearn-statsmodels`](../corpus/sklearn-statsmodels).
  - Feature matrix standardization (z-score: mean 0, variance 1).
  - Ordinary Least Squares regression solving $(X^T X)^{-1} X^T y$.
  - 2-sided Gaussian p-value calculations matching SciPy.
- **`quantanalysis`**:
  - Simple Moving Average (SMA), Exponential Moving Average (EMA).
  - Bollinger Bands ($\mu \pm 2\sigma$).
  - Historical Maximum Drawdown percentage computation.
- **`edatools`**:
  - Five-number summary (Min, Q1, Median, Q3, Max).
  - Tukey's fences interquartile range (IQR) outlier detection.
  - Sample Fisher-Pearson skewness coefficient.
- **`haversine` (PyPI v0.1)**:
  - Great-circle distance calculations between coordinate pairs across kilometers and miles.
  - Transpiled and executed with exact geographic precision.
- **`geoprojector`**:
  - Spherical Web Mercator forward and inverse coordinate projections.
  - Planar polygon centroid determination.
  - Spatial density binning and grid counts.
- **Demography & Population Modeling**:
  - Logistic growth curve modeling.
  - Leslie matrix multi-age cohort projection.
  - Youth, elderly, and total demographic dependency ratios.
